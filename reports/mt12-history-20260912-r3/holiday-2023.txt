Subject:      Hong Kong Securities Market Holiday Schedule for Year 2023
Enquiry:      Participant General Enquiry Hotline 1 (Tel : 2840 3626 E-mail : trd@hkex.com.hk)

Exchange Participants are advised that the Hong Kong securities market holiday schedule for Year
2023 has been prepared in the following table according to the general holidays for Year 2023 as
announced by the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region. Holiday
information is also available on the HKEX Calendar at http://www.hkex.com.hk/News/HKEX-
Calendar?sc_lang=en.


Securities Market is closed on Saturdays, Sundays and Public Holidays as specified below:

           Date                       Day                                  Holiday
        2 January 2023             Monday            The day following the first day of January
      23 January 2023              Monday            The second day of Lunar New Year
      24 January 2023              Tuesday           The third day of Lunar New Year
      25 January 2023            Wednesday           The fourth day of Lunar New Year
            5 April 2023         Wednesday           Ching Ming Festival
            7 April 2023             Friday          Good Friday
           10 April 2023           Monday            Easter Monday
             1 May 2023            Monday            Labour Day
           26 May 2023               Friday          The Birthday of the Buddha
           22 June 2023            Thursday          Tuen Ng Festival
        2 October 2023             Monday            The day following National Day
      23 October 2023              Monday            Chung Yeung Festival
    25 December 2023               Monday            Christmas Day
    26 December 2023               Tuesday           The first weekday after Christmas Day

There is no half-day trading day in Year 2023.
