weather_2106283 Market Operations 28 Jun 2021 Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited (HKEX) confirms that trading in its securities and derivatives markets, including Stock Connect trading, will resume at 1:30 pm today (Monday), following the cancellation of the Black Rainstorm Warning.
