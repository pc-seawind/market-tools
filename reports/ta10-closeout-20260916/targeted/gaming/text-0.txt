Top 10 Worldwide Mobile Games By Revenue and Downloads in August 2026
Honor of Kings maintained its position as the world’s highest-grossing mobile game in August 2026. Gossip Harbor, Whiteout Survival, Royal Match, and Candy Crush Saga rounded out the global top five.
According to Sensor Tower’s App Performance Insights,
Honor of Kings
maintained its lead in global mobile game revenue in August. In China, the game launched two major events from August 8–15: the
Summer Farm Friends Festival
and the
Endless Game Parallel World
skin series. The update introduced the new hero
LuYana
, the limited-time
Extreme Frenzy
mode featuring shorter cooldowns and stackable upgrades, free draws, new cosmetics, and event-exclusive rewards to drive engagement and spending.
Gossip Harbor
delivered another standout performance, supported by a carefully paced live-ops calendar.
Water Wonders
and
Dollhouse of Dreams
carried momentum from July into the first half of the month, before
Fourth-Year Festivities
celebrated the game’s fourth anniversary with gala-themed merge objectives and exclusive rewards. The dragon-rescue storyline
Slay the Dragon, Save the Day!
sustained engagement through late August, while
Escape from Raccoon Beach
and the autumn-themed
The Turning Leaves
provided fresh collection goals extending into September.
Other top-grossing games maintained engagement and spending through a steady flow of themed events.
Whiteout Survival
delivered a packed August calendar led by its
Kingshot
collaboration
. Its centerpiece,
Twin Star Adventure
, took players on a cooperative, story-driven polar bear expedition, while alliance competitions such as
State of Power
and
Winter Siege
kept groups competing throughout the month.
Royal Match
featured
Dragon Nest
and the timed
Lightning Rush
competition during August, adding cooperative and competitive formats to its broader live-ops calendar. Meanwhile,
Candy Crush Saga
maintained engagement through a well-paced sequence of content, moving from
Bubblegum Season
into the multi-round
Candy Cup 2026
tournament before
Music Season
arrived at the end of August.
For the full ranking of the top 10 mobile games by revenue, please see the chart above. Please note that data from third-party Android markets is excluded.
Global consumer spending on mobile games across the App Store and Google Play reached
$6.57 billion in August
, down
1% month-over-month
. The
United States
accounted for 28.6% of total revenue, followed by
China (iOS only)
at 16.7% and
Japan
at 12.2%.
On the revenue growth chart,
Fate/Grand Order
, a turn-based RPG, ranked No. 1 worldwide. Growth was driven primarily by Japan, where the game recorded its highest monthly revenue of 2026 in August. Its
11th Anniversary celebration
offered daily login rewards, new missions, special challenges, and limited-time character draws led by the
newly introduced Hassan of the Azrael
. The annual
Swimsuit Event 2026
followed with summer-themed outfits and new collectible characters, helping sustain player spending into early September.
Tencent-owned or published games claimed five of the top 10 spots by worldwide revenue growth in August, led by
Teamfight Tactics
and
VALORANT
at No. 2 and No. 3, respectively. Growth was driven primarily by China. The Chinese version of
Teamfight Tactics
introduced the
Force of Nature
season
, which refreshed the playable characters and strategic options, alongside a premium
Gwen character cosmetic
with two interchangeable designs.
VALORANT
released two themed weapon collections: the traditional Chinese art-inspired
Ink Warrior
and the floral
Thousand Spirits
, launched as part of the mobile game’s first-anniversary celebrations.
Game for Peace
,
League of Legends: Wild Rift
, and
Delta Force
also climbed the rankings.
Game for Peace
offered characters, weapons, and vehicles from the animated series
Swallowed Star
, while
Wild Rift
combined
fourth-anniversary rewards
with new
Crystal Rose 2026
character outfits
.
Delta Force’s
Treasure Month
encouraged players to log in, complete matches, and hunt for valuable in-game collectibles. Together, these campaigns show how Tencent used major updates, anniversary celebrations, popular entertainment collaborations, and limited-edition cosmetics to bring players back and create new spending opportunities throughout August.
Ranking No. 10 on the worldwide revenue growth chart, newcomer
Zeus: The God of Arrogance
has held the No. 1 position by mobile game revenue in South Korea since its late-August launch. Published by Com2uS Holdings, the competitive MMORPG reimagines Greek mythology through large-scale battles, class-based combat, and competitive modes designed for players at different progression levels. It also features an offline system that continues developing characters while players are away and a crafting-focused class that can produce exclusive items, supporting both long-term progression and the in-game economy.
Overall, August’s results showed that effective live ops is as much about pacing as scale. Leading games combined major updates, anniversaries, and IP collaborations with frequent rewards, limited-time modes, premium cosmetics, and locally relevant celebrations, giving players recurring reasons to return and spend throughout the month. Meanwhile, fast-rising newcomers demonstrated that well-positioned launches can still break through a competitive market. Explore
Sensor Tower Playliner
for deeper insights into the live-ops strategies shaping monetization and player engagement.
Top 10 Worldwide Mobile Games Download Rankings for August 2026
ROBLOX
was the most downloaded mobile game worldwide in August 2026, followed by
Free Fire
at No. 2.
Block Blast!
,
Subway Surfers
, and
Arrow Puzzle: Tap Puzzle Games
rounded out the top five.
ROBLOX
’s No. 1 position was supported by a diverse lineup of featured experiences throughout August. These included the
Vesteria
MMORPG and tactical FPS
TTK
, alongside major updates for
Dress to Impress
and
Tower Defense Simulator
.
Duck Duck
added a social party game to the lineup. This steady flow of fresh content across role-playing, shooter, fashion, strategy, and casual gameplay broadened Roblox’s appeal and helped sustain interest in the platform.
Free Fire
retained the No. 2 position by worldwide downloads in August, carrying momentum from its
ninth-anniversary celebration
, which concluded in late July. Following a quieter start to the month,
Scarlet Moon
launched across
Free Fire
and
Free Fire MAX
in late August, refreshing the returning collection with new designs and exclusive rewards. The game’s continued strength highlights its sustained global appeal and the lasting momentum generated by its anniversary campaign.
Subway Surfers
climbed three positions month over month to rank No. 4 by worldwide downloads in August. Its
Fantasy Fest
World Tour update introduced an enchanted setting, new playable characters, and limited-time challenges, including a back-to-school event with collectible rewards. The late-month
Cosmic Crossroads
update shifted the game into a space-themed environment, adding new characters, seasonal reward tracks, and the return of the
Among Us
collaboration
. This steady rotation of themed locations, collectible characters, challenges, and crossover content helped keep the game fresh and broaden its appeal to new and returning players.
Global mobile game downloads reached
3.76 billion
in August, up
1% month-over-month
.
India
led with
614 million downloads (16.4%)
, followed by the
United States (7.4%)
and
Brazil (5.8%)
.
Open-world crime games emerged as a notable download trend in August, led by two fast-rising mobile titles.
Gangstar Mirage City
ranked No. 1 by worldwide download growth and reached No. 7 by total downloads following its
August 20 multi-region rollout
. Published by Level Infinite in partnership with Gameloft, the game lets players explore a futuristic city, customize vehicles, build criminal empires, and compete in heists and gang battles.
Gangster Miami RP: Grand City
ranked No. 6 by download growth, offering a similar mix of urban exploration, driving, and crime-themed missions. Growth across both titles was supported by high-volume and fast-growing mobile gaming markets such as India, Indonesia, Pakistan, and Brazil. Their rise highlights strong demand for accessible open-world crime games on mobile and coincided with growing anticipation for
Grand Theft Auto VI
. Rockstar added to the buzz with an
extended preview
in late August, ahead of the game’s November 19 launch.
Newcomer
Mystery Dumpling: Unbox ASMR
ranked No. 2 by worldwide download growth and reached No. 12 by total downloads following its August launch. Published by Brocker Bus Studio, the ultracasual title combines mystery-box unwrapping, ASMR-inspired interactions, and colorful cartoon visuals. Its rapid growth was led by Indonesia, Brazil, and Turkey, driving more than 11 million downloads within its launch month.
Vehicle-themed games gained momentum in August, with
Bus Fever-Car Jam Escape
,
Dear Passengers
, and
Wheelie Master: Moto Ride 3D
all entering the top 10 by worldwide download growth. The titles offered distinct casual takes on transportation through bus-sorting puzzles, passenger aviation simulation, and motorcycle stunt riding. Their performance highlights the broad appeal of simple, vehicle-based gameplay across high-volume markets such as India, Indonesia, Brazil, Mexico, and Turkey.
Overall, August’s download rankings balanced the staying power of established global hits with rapid growth from new and emerging titles. Frequent content updates helped leading games remain visible, while accessible concepts spanning open-world crime, ASMR, puzzles, and vehicle simulation found momentum across high-volume mobile markets.
